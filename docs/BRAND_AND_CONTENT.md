## Recommended detailed case study structure

1. **Context** — who needed it and what problem mattered.
2. **My role** — what you personally designed, built, wrote, operated or led.
3. **Approach** — tradeoffs, architecture, design and operational decisions.
4. **Artifacts** — screenshots, architecture diagrams, code snippets, campaigns or real documents.
5. **Outcome** — what is independently verifiable; if metrics are unknown, say what was delivered.
6. **Current status** — Live / Client deliverable / Prototype / In development / Concept.
7. **Next** — where the product or experiment is heading, only if pertinent.
